What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only on your device and never sent to us:

  • your collection (cards, languages, variants, quantities, trade settings)
  • price alerts and the history of your collection's value
  • your settings (languages, currency, price source, view options)
  • a local copy of card data, prices and images, so the app also works offline
  • the number of free entries you have used and whether you bought the full version

We have no server and no access to any of this. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too, except for backup files (see below).

What leaves your phone

Camera, notifications and backups

Camera. The camera is used only while you scan. Text on the card is recognised on your phone; photos are not saved and not uploaded.

Notifications. If you set price alerts and allow notifications, the app checks prices once a day in the background and notifies you on your phone. No notification service of ours is involved.

Backups. The app saves a backup of your collection to the folder “Documents/BinderBuddy” on your phone, keeping the last 7 days. These files stay on your phone, even if you delete the app, and you can delete them at any time. When you export your collection, you choose where the file goes, for example Google Drive; that service's privacy policy then applies.

Purchases

The full version and optional tips are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether this Google account has bought the full version and stores the answer on your phone.

No tracking, no ads

BinderBuddy contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools. It does not need an account and does not collect contact details, location or contacts. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Because we store no data about you, we usually have nothing to provide or delete; your collection is under your control on your phone. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.
